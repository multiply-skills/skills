# Award criteria: starting points

**Always check this year's entry kit on the show's own website.** Questions, word limits and
weightings change every year and vary by category. Use this page only to orient yourself
before you have the kit.

| Show | What juries reward | Typical weighting (verify) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cannes Lions: Creative Effectiveness** | Proven, long-term business impact of work that has already won at Cannes | Idea 25%, Strategy 25%, Results 50% | The work must have been shortlisted or won at an earlier Cannes Lions. Juries penalise over-claiming. Source: canneslions.com, "Creative Effectiveness: what you need to know" |
| **Cannes Lions: other Lions** (Film, Outdoor, Social & Creator, etc.) | Idea, execution and craft for that discipline; impact and results matter more in some Lions | Varies by Lion | Each Lion publishes its own criteria and weightings |
| **Effie Awards** | Marketing effectiveness: objectives, insight, strategy, execution, and results that prove impact | Commonly: Challenge/Context/Objectives 23.3%, Insights & Strategic Idea 23.3%, Bringing the Idea to Life 23.3%, Results 30% | Weights differ between national Effie programmes. Results must be tied to the stated objectives |
| **D&AD** | Creative and craft excellence. Pencils are rare by design | Not a fixed numeric weighting | Short written statements; the work itself carries the entry |
| **The One Show** | Creative excellence across disciplines | Varies by discipline | Short written fields; strong case films matter |
| **Epica** | Creative excellence, judged by journalists | Varies | European focus; concise written fields |

## Common entry fields
Background · Challenge / Objectives · Insight · Strategy · Idea · Execution · Results · Supporting
materials (case film, images, URL) · Client confirmation of results.

## Evidence juries look for
- Objectives set **before** the campaign, with baselines
- Business results (sales, share, penetration, price premium, profit), not only reach
- A comparison: before/after, control markets, or modelling
- Other factors (price, distribution, seasonality, competitor activity) explicitly ruled out
- Sources and time periods for every number
