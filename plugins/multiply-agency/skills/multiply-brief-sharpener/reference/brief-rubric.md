# Brief rubric

Score each criterion 0–2 (0 = missing, 1 = present but vague, 2 = clear and specific).
Total out of 20. Quote or paraphrase the evidence for each score.

| # | Criterion | 2 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Background / why now** | A specific trigger: launch, share loss, new competitor, repositioning |
| 2 | **Business objective** | A commercial outcome with a number and a date |
| 3 | **Communication objective** | What people should think, feel or do differently |
| 4 | **Audience** | A defined person with a need or tension, plus their current beliefs |
| 5 | **Insight** | Something true and non-obvious about the audience that the work can use |
| 6 | **Proposition** | One single-minded thought, not a list of benefits |
| 7 | **Reasons to believe** | 2–4 concrete proof points |
| 8 | **Deliverables & channels** | Formats, markets, quantities, or explicitly open |
| 9 | **Budget & timing** | A working budget range and key dates |
| 10 | **Success measures** | KPIs with baselines and how they will be measured |

## Reading the total
- **16–20:** a strong brief. Tighten the wording and confirm the details.
- **10–15:** workable. Resolve the "must answer" questions before creative work starts.
- **0–9:** not a brief yet. Propose a briefing call and send the questions first.

## Common red flags
- Several objectives with no priority ("awareness, consideration and sales")
- An audience of "everyone" or a broad age band
- A proposition that is the product description
- Mandatories that contradict the objective
- A budget or timeline that doesn't match the ambition
- A creative solution already written into the brief ("we need a TikTok challenge")
